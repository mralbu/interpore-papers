        Meetings

          Who comes back

          Distinct author names per meeting, split by whether the name appeared in an earlier meeting with abstracts (from 2016). Names are matched as written, so a changed spelling counts as a new author.

          Themes over time

          Share of each meeting's abstracts whose title or abstract mentions the theme (simple keyword match; each panel has its own scale).

          Topics by year

          Share of each meeting's abstracts in each map topic, topics ordered by the year they peaked.

          Sources: InterPore events, the meeting pages on interpore.org and events.interpore.org. The abstracts belong to their authors.
